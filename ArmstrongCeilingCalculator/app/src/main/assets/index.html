Schematic layout preview. Main runners run along the room length. 1200 mm cross tees are placed at 600 mm intervals across every bay, including perimeter bays. Balanced Centered bisects each bay with 600 mm tees. Corner Bound preserves a 600 mm module in an edge bay wider than 600 mm and leaves any remainder at the wall; no green tee is added when an edge bay is 600 mm or narrower. Perimeter pieces may need cutting to suit room dimensions. Confirm manufacturer-specific connector details on site.
